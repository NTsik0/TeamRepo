## 2. Pattern journal, Weeks 1 to 4

Copied from [`pattern-journal.md`](pattern-journal.md).

### Week 1 · Act like an owner, not a role

**Where I applied it:** Nobody gave me a problem, so I started looking around me. I wrote down three things I saw myself: my friend needed C# but could not take it at KIU when he needed it, so he took an outside course; my friend Bachi and my parents in Batumi park far from home and walk about 10 minutes every time; and in villages almost every family makes wine, while restaurants buy imported wine.

**Agency beyond my lane:** The parking problem was not a guess. For an earlier software homework I asked people what their main problem was, and parking kept coming up.

**What I hacked together:** I just asked my parents and friends what annoys them every day, before we had any interview script.

**Honest note:** My first ideas were apps, not problems ("a parking app", "a subscription app"). I had to learn to write them as who / struggle / cost. File: `00-foundation/problem-pool.md` (N1 to N3).

### Week 2 · Evidence over opinion

**Where I applied it:** Guram and I became a team and put our six ideas together. When we looked at them again, most were solutions, not problems we had seen. We had not checked any of them with real people yet.

**Agency beyond my lane:** I wrote down Guram's three ideas too, so the whole pool was in one place.

**What I hacked together:** Nothing yet. We talked about the ideas but did not test anything. Files: `00-foundation/problem-pool.md`, `00-foundation/team-contract.md`.

### Week 3 · Kill your favorite idea

**Where I applied it:** We had our repo (still empty) and started brainstorming about the ideas. My favorite was village wine, but we started to not like it, because we could not find anyone interested in it. We did not drop it officially yet; that happened when we scored it in Week 4.

**Agency beyond my lane:** The professor was travelling, so nobody pushed us. I talked to a friend about courses on my own.

**What I hacked together:** Before any script, I just asked a friend about learning outside university. He told me how hard it was to find courses and that it took a lot of time. This made me take the courses problem more seriously than wine. It became our pick: `00-foundation/four-filters-scorecard.md`.

**Honest note:** We still had no hypothesis and no real interviews by the end of Week 3. This is the week we fell behind.

### Week 4 · The prototype is the conversation

**Where I applied it:** Honestly, not yet. In Week 4 we were still catching up on discovery, so we did not put anything working in front of a student. On Friday 9 October we scored our ideas with the Four Filters: wine, my favorite, got 3 of 8 because we could not reach farmers or restaurants (0 on reach); courses got 8, so we picked it and kept wine as runner-up. This was "kill your favorite idea" from Week 3, done one week late. Files: `00-foundation/four-filters-scorecard.md`, `DECISIONS.md` line 1.

**Agency beyond my lane:** I set up the whole team repo and moved our work into it. I wrote our problem hypothesis and committed it before any interview, so the commit date proves we guessed first (`01-discovery/problem-hypothesis.md`), and wrote the interview script in Georgian and English (`01-discovery/interview-script-v1.md`).

**What I hacked together:** To score "reach" I did not guess. I listed real names: Nini, Gio, Beka, Luka and Giorgi for courses; Bachi, my parents, Giorgi and Gaga for parking. The names decided the score. The first working thing I made came just after Week 4: the Google Form I use during interviews (`docs/create-interview-form.gs`), and then our prototype plan (`02-design/prototype-plan.md`).

**DECISIONS.md lines I drove:** line 1 (problem pick).
