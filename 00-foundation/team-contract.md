## How we work
- **Channel:** our team Discord server (calls and screen sharing); Microsoft Teams for course announcements; in person in the dorm room.
- **Meetings:** Saturday and Sunday 8:00 to 11:30; Monday and Tuesday 9:00 to 11:00.
- **Response window:** messages acknowledged within 12 hours between 9:00 and 23:00.
- **Silent teammate with a deadline near:** the other member follows up on Discord after 12 hours, then calls or knocks on the door; after 48 hours of silence before a deadline, we raise it with the professor.
- **Demo-ready:** any member can screen-share the current product state in any lab, on 2 minutes notice.

## How we track who did what
- Everyone commits their own work from their own GitHub account; the commit history is the record.
- Every line in `DECISIONS.md` names an owner by initials (NTS or GTS).
- Every interview log names the asker and the logger by initials.
- Each of us keeps `members/<github-username>/pattern-journal.md`, written the week it happens.
- AI-assisted work is logged in `docs/ai-usage-log.md` with who used it.

## How we decide
Small stuff: either of us. Direction: evidence first; with two members, if we still disagree, the evidence in the interview logs decides, and the dissent gets one sentence in `DECISIONS.md`. From Week 3, product decisions go in `DECISIONS.md` with a link to their evidence. That file is the team memory; keep it honest.

## When it breaks
1. Name it in the team channel within 48 hours of noticing.
2. Still stuck after one meeting: raise it at a lab checkpoint.
3. Step 2 failed: email zeshan.ahmad@kiu.edu.ge for mediation.
