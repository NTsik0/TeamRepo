# Ideal Customer Profile

One person, not a segment. v1, written before any real interview.

**Name (invented) and one-line sketch:** Sandro, 20, a KIU computer science student who needs a skill (like C#) before the university teaches it.

**Their situation:** Studies at KIU in Kutaisi and lives in or near the dorm. Has a plan for the next year or two (a project, an internship, a portfolio) that needs specific skills. Learns from KIU courses, YouTube, and online course sites, and talks with friends about which courses and certificates are worth it.

**The moment the problem shows up:** When he needs a specific skill for a project, a job application, or a next course, and finds out that KIU does not teach it this semester, or he cannot get into it yet.

**What it costs them:** Waiting means losing a semester. Not waiting means spending his own evenings (and sometimes money) finding, choosing and finishing an outside course, on top of the KIU workload. *(To confirm with numbers in interviews.)*

**What they do about it today:** Searches online for a course, asks friends which one is good, and takes it on his own, the way our friend did with C#. Some aim for a certificate to put in a portfolio.

**What they are NOT:**
- Students who are happy with only the KIU curriculum and never look outside it: they do not have the problem.
- People already working full time who take courses for a career change: different moment, different budget, and we cannot reach five of them this week.
