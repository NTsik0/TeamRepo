## The script you will read from

1. Tell me about the last time you learned something new outside the university, from the start.
2. Who told you about that course? How did it reach you?
3. How long did it take from when you started looking until you signed up?
4. What did it cost you in time and money? How many hours, how many lari?
5. Tell me about the last free course you heard about too late, or almost missed. What happened?
6. What did you do with the course or certificate after you finished?
7. Who else should I talk to about this?

Follow-ups: And then what happened? · Why was that a problem? · When was the last time? · Can you show me where you looked?
