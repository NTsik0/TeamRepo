# Interview 10 · Computer science student, 2nd year · (code name: Gio-2)

**Week:** 7 · **Date:** 2 November 2026 · **Asker:** NTS · **Logger:** NTS · **Consent:** yes; notes only
**Real or practice:** real · **Recorded with:** interview script v2 (`01-discovery/interview-script-v2.md`), in person, plus the new question on how long registration stays open; answers written down by NTS during the conversation.

**Context:** Second-year computer science student, not the Gio in our outreach tracker. Took a free Coursera Java course last year after a friend who had taken it a year earlier told him about it; 200 to 300 hours in total.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if students do not describe, before we mention it, a specific time they needed a skill that KIU was not teaching them yet.

## Answers (★ = surprise)

- **Last thing learned outside university:** "last year, I learned Java outside the university. It was really good experience and It was one of the coursera's courses."
- **Who told him:** "my friend told me about that, he was already attending one a year before"
- **From looking to signing up:** "about a month or a little bit more I guess."
- **Cost:** "Maybe around 200-300 hours totally. And it was free."
- **A free course he heard about too late or almost missed:** "this year I missed coursera's course because I forgot to check it on time and the deadline was already over when I checked." ★
- **How long registration was open, and how he found out it had closed (new question):** "it was open for 15-30 days. After registering I got mail notification about ending and passing to the next level." ★
- **What he did with the certificate:** "I placed it on my linkedin"
- **Who else should we talk to:** "my friend Niko also had same course."
- **Can we come back:** "sure"

## Summary

**Workaround:** A friend who had taken the course a year earlier passed it on; for the course he missed this year, he relied on remembering to check Coursera himself.
**Cost:** 0 GEL, 200 to 300 hours of study, about a month from looking to signing up. This year he missed a Coursera course because the deadline had passed when he checked.
**Frequency:** One completed outside course; one missed.
**Prediction check:** Not raised. He never mentioned KIU or a subject the university was missing.
**Surprise ★:** Registration was open for 15 to 30 days, not a day or two, and he still missed it because he "forgot to check it on time". The window is not short; the problem is remembering to look inside it. Once registered, Coursera emailed him about deadlines; before registering, nothing reminded him.
**Commitment:** Can come back later; suggested his friend Niko, who took the same course.
**Next:** Ask Niko. Ask the next students whether the course they missed had a reminder before registration, and how long it was open.
