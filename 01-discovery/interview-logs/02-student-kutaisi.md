# Interview 02 · Management student, 3rd year · (code name: Ana)

**Week:** 5 · **Date:** 12 October 2026 · **Asker:** NTS · **Logger:** NTS · **Consent:** yes; notes only
**Real or practice:** real

**Context:** Third-year management student who wants to start working in marketing. About half a year ago she took a free TBC course, found through a friend. Her last certificate came from RiHEI, a course the university told her about. (RiHEI: "Responsible and Impactful Universities as Sustainable Growth and Enterprise Catalysts in Georgia", an EU Erasmus+ funded project to make Georgian universities hubs of sustainable growth and job creation.)
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if students do not describe, before we mention it, a specific time they needed a skill that KIU was not teaching them yet.

## Questions and answers (Georgian as spoken; English beside it; ★ = surprise)

**1. როგორ სწავლობ ახალ უნარებს ყოველდღიურად?** *How do you learn new skills day to day?*
- (no answer recorded)

**2. რას სწავლობ და რა პროფესიით გინდა მუშაობის დაწყება?** *What do you study, and what job do you want to start?*
- "მენეჯმენტს, მუშაობის დაწყება მინდა მარკეტინგის განხრით" / "Management; I want to start working in marketing."

**3. მომიყევი, ბოლოს როდის ისწავლე რამე ახალი უნივერსიტეტის გარეთ.** *Tell me about the last time you learned something new outside the university.*
- "თიბისის კურსზე ნახევარი წლის წინ" / "At a TBC course, half a year ago."

**4. რისთვის დაგჭირდა ეს მაშინ?** *What did you need it for at that time?*

**6. ოდესმე მიგიტოვებია ან ვერ დაგისრულებია მსგავსი კურსი?** *Have you ever dropped or not finished a course like that?*
- "არა" / "No."

**7. სად იპოვე ეს კურსი და როგორ აირჩიე?** *Where did you find the course and how did you choose it?*
- "მეგობარმა ჩამიგდო და ერთად ავარჩიეთ ჩვენი ინტერესის მიხედვით" / "A friend sent it to me, and we chose it together based on our interests." ★

**8. მომიყევი ბოლო სერტიფიკატზე, რომელიც აიღე.** *Tell me about the last certificate you got.*
- "ბოლოს ავიღე რიჰეის სერტიფიკატი, უნივერსიტეტმა გამიზიარა ინფორმაცია ამ კურსის შესახებ" / "The last one I got was a RiHEI certificate; the university shared information about this course with me." ★

**Interviewer note: ასწავლიდა უნივერსიტეტი იმ უნარს, რაც თქვენ აიღეთ, იმ დონით რაც გსურდათ?** *Was the university offering the same subject at the level you wanted?*
- No.

## Summary

**Workaround:** Free courses (TBC), found through a friend; certificate courses the university announces (RiHEI).
**Cost:** 0 GEL; about 48 hours (2 hours, twice a week, for 3 months).
**Frequency:** One outside course half a year ago; nothing new since.
**Prediction check:** Only after we asked. On her own she said she took the course "only to deepen my knowledge"; when we asked directly, she said the university did not teach that skill at the level she wanted.
**Surprise ★:** She did not search at all: a friend sent her the course. The university itself pointed her to a certificate course, so KIU is a source of outside courses, not only the reason for them. Unlike Beka, there is no job pressure and no new course since.
**Commitment:** Said we can come back when we have something to show.
**Next:** Ask the next students who told them about the course (a friend, the university, an ad) and whether they would have found it alone.
