# Affinity Map

Every quote from every real log, grouped by what the quotes have in common. A cluster counts as a pattern only when it holds quotes from 2 or more different interviews.

**Logs in this map:** 01 to 07. Re-sorted after logs 03 to 06 (18 October): the old "channels" cluster split in two, because three students named a course they had missed, which is a cost, not a channel.

## Pattern 1

**Pattern:** Students take free outside courses to go deeper or get job-ready; almost none say KIU was missing the subject.

**Interviews behind it:** 01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 06, 07 (1 of 7 chose "the university did not teach it then", among four other reasons: 07)

**Quotes:**
- "ვეძებდი კურსებს რომ მესწავლა ის რაც მინდოდა პარალელურად უნივერსიტეტის დროს." / "I was looking for courses to learn what I wanted, in parallel with university." (01)
- "ვცდილობ რომ სამსახური ვიპოვო მალე და მაქსიმალურად იყენებს ყველა შესაძლებლობას" / "I'm trying to find a job soon and [I] use every opportunity as much as possible." (01)
- "მხოლოდ ჩემი ცოდნის გასაღრმავებლად" / "Only to deepen my knowledge." (02)
- "I was looking for an excel course to deepen my knowledge, and I found it on Coursera." (06)
- Reasons chosen: job 03, 05, 07; going deeper 03, 04, 06, 07; certificate 03, 04, 07; a friend was taking it 04, 07; the university did not teach it then 07.

## Pattern 2

**Interviews behind it:** 01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 06, 07

**Quotes:**

- "კვირაში ორჯერ, 2 საათი, 3 თვის განმავლობაში. უფასო იყო" / "Twice a week, 2 hours, for 3 months. It was free." (02)

- "...where I found it it costed money." (07; paid courses were easy to find, free ones were not)

**Interviews behind it:** 03, 05, 06, 07 (4 of 7; 01 and 04 said "Never")

**Quotes:**

- "About a year before I missed another tbc course" (05)
- "I have visited the website but it was like a day or two days late so I registration was cancelled" (05, follow-up 19 Oct)
- "under course, it said registration date expired yesterday" (05, follow-up 19 Oct)
- "TBC Risk Academy" (06): "found from their website, but it was showing registration no more active" (06)

- Time from starting to look to signing up: more than a month (01 follow-up, 07); 1 to 7 days (05, 06).
